VALIDATION LOGIC
Use these source-of-truth rules:

   - Period covered
   - Submission type
2. Compare against company master data:
   - SEC registration number
   - Company name
   - Period covered years

- spacing differences
- line breaks
- “Inc.” vs “Corp.”
