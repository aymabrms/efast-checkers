PROBLEM

The current completeness evaluator searches the full text of every page.

As a result, an Independent Auditor's Report containing wording such as:

"the financial statements comprise the statements of financial position, income,
comprehensive income, changes in equity and cash flows"

can incorrectly cause those actual financial statement components to be marked as Detected.

This is not acceptable for the completeness check.

BUSINESS RULE

A page that merely REFERENCES another financial statement must not count as proof that the actual statement is present.

--------------------------------------------------
1. EXCLUDE AUDITOR REPORT FROM OTHER COMPONENT EVIDENCE
--------------------------------------------------

When evaluating these components:

- Statement of Financial Position / Balance Sheet
- Statement of Profit or Loss / Statement of Income
- Other Comprehensive Income
- Statement of Changes in Equity
- Statement of Cash Flows

Do NOT use a page classified as:

Independent Auditor's Report

as strong or weak evidence for those components.

The Auditor's Report itself should still count normally for:

Independent Auditor's Report.

Do not remove or alter the Auditor's Report page classification.

--------------------------------------------------
2. PREFER ACTUAL STATEMENT EVIDENCE
--------------------------------------------------

For the five financial statement components above, mark Detected primarily when:

A. the page classification exactly corresponds to that financial statement;

OR

B. the page contains strong statement-heading evidence and supporting financial-statement content.

Examples:

Financial Position:
- "Statement of Financial Position"
- "Balance Sheet"
plus supporting terms such as assets / liabilities / equity

Income:
- "Statement of Income"
- "Statement of Profit or Loss"
- "Income Statement"

Comprehensive Income:
- "Statement of Comprehensive Income"
- "Other Comprehensive Income"

Changes in Equity:
- "Statement of Changes in Equity"
- equivalent strong heading already supported by current rules

Cash Flows:
- "Statement of Cash Flows"
plus cash-flow activity terms where available

Do NOT require fixed page numbers.
Page order must remain flexible.

--------------------------------------------------
3. KEEP COMBINED INCOME / OCI SUPPORT
--------------------------------------------------

A legitimate combined page such as:

"Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income"

must still satisfy both:
- Statement of Profit or Loss / Statement of Income
- Other Comprehensive Income

Do not require a separate OCI page.

--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------

Do not change:
- Statement of Management's Responsibility detection
- Auditor's Report detection
- Notes detection
- Proof of BIR Filing behavior
- completeness table UI
- reviewer recommendation logic
