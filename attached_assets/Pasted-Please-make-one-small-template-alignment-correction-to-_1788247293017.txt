==================================================

GIS Review Lite currently treats Corporate Secretary / Notarization as optional/informational in GIS completeness.

For BOTH:

- GIS Stock
- GIS Non-Stock

Corporate Secretary Attestation / Notarization must be a REQUIRED GIS completeness component.

Use existing text evidence such as:

- Corporate Secretary
- declare under penalty of perjury
- hereby attest
- SUBSCRIBED AND SWORN
- ACKNOWLEDGMENT
- ACKNOWLEDGEMENT
- NOTARY PUBLIC
- DOC. NO.
- PAGE NO.
- BOOK NO.
- SERIES OF

Business behavior:

A. If the attestation/notarization section is not detected:
- Component = Missing
- GIS Completeness = Failed

B. If appropriate attestation/notarial text is detected:
- Component = Detected

Continue showing separately:

Signature / Notarial Stamp:
Visual Confirmation Required

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT claim automated verification of:
- Corporate Secretary signature
- Notary signature
- stamp
- seal
- authenticity
- legal sufficiency

The required completeness rule only verifies that the expected attestation/notarization component appears to be present.

3. KEEP BOD LOGIC UNCHANGED
==================================================

The 2020 GIS templates place a Beneficial Ownership Declaration after the normal GIS body.

The BOD page explicitly contains wording equivalent to:

"This page is not for uploading on the SEC iView."

Therefore preserve the existing behavior:

- BOD = Conditional / Legacy
- detect separately
- detected page may be selected for routing
- excluded from GIS completeness PASS/FAIL
- absence does not fail GIS
- accepted QA copy may legitimately contain no BOD
- no substantive BOD validation
- no actual external forwarding

Do NOT make BOD required.
