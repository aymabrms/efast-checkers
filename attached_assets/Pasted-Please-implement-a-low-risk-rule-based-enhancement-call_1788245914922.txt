4. ACCEPTED QR / COVER PAGE HANDLING
==================================================

Some already-accepted GIS reports may have an SEC QR/acceptance page appended as the first page.

Detect likely QR/acceptance-cover pages using available text evidence where reasonably possible.

Possible indicators may include:
- SEC acceptance wording;
- QR Code / QR-related text;
- Accepted / Received filing information;
- filing/reference metadata not matching normal GIS body labels.

When identified:

- classify it as:
  "SEC Acceptance / QR Cover"

- exclude it from GIS completeness requirements;
- do not treat it as the Corporate/Profile page;
- do not allow its page position to shift or break GIS component detection.

Do NOT claim visual QR-code authentication.

If detection is uncertain:
- leave the page reviewable;
- do not fail completeness because of it.

6. BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP DECLARATION (BOD)
==================================================

IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE:

Beneficial Ownership Declaration is a CONDITIONAL / LEGACY routing component.

It must NOT be treated as universally required for all GIS files.

Older/legacy GIS templates, including versions still allowed during transition/extension periods, may include a BOD page.

Newer GIS forms may not include it.

Also, during eFAST processing:

- the receiving personnel may select the page number containing the BOD;
- that BOD page may be forwarded/routed to another department;
- after GIS acceptance, the BOD page may no longer appear in the accepted QA copy;
- accepted reports may instead contain an SEC QR cover page.

Therefore:

A. Detect possible BOD page evidence using phrases such as:

- BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP DECLARATION
- DECLARATION OF BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP
- BENEFICIAL OWNER
- BENEFICIAL OWNERS

Only use strong, explicit BOD heading/evidence.

Do NOT classify an ordinary page merely because the phrase "beneficial owner" appears incidentally.

B. Show a separate GIS review item:

"Beneficial Ownership Declaration"

Possible statuses:

- Detected
- Not Detected
- Needs Review
- Not Applicable / Accepted QA Copy

C. Show detected page number(s).

Example:

Beneficial Ownership Declaration | Detected | Page 8

D. Add a lightweight reviewer control if simple:

"BOD Page for Routing"

Dropdown:
- None / Not Applicable
- Page 1
- Page 2
- ...
- Page N

If an explicit BOD page is confidently detected:
- preselect that page if safe.

Reviewer must remain able to change the selection.

Do NOT automatically transmit or route any document externally.
This is only a prototype representation of the current receiving workflow.

E. BOD must be excluded from the normal GIS completeness PASS/FAIL calculation.

BOD absence alone must NEVER make GIS Completeness = Failed in this V1.

F. Accepted/QA copy handling:

If an SEC Acceptance / QR Cover page is detected:
- BOD absence should be displayed as:
  "Not Applicable / Accepted QA Copy"
  or another equivalent reviewer-friendly status.

Do not expect the BOD to remain in an accepted GIS copy.

G. Do NOT hard-code a specific template year requirement.

If form-version evidence such as 2020 or 2026 can be detected safely:
- surface it as supporting evidence only.

Do not fail a filing solely because the template version cannot be determined.

7. GIS PERIOD YEAR VALIDATION
==================================================

Detect the GIS heading:

"FOR THE YEAR ____"

or equivalent clear heading evidence.

Compare against Intake -> Period Year.

Example:

Detected:
FOR THE YEAR 2026

Intake:
Period Year = 2026

Result:
Passed.

If clearly mismatched:
Failed.

If not confidently detected:
Needs Review.
